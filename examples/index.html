HOW IT WORKS

From PDF to typed JSON in three lines.

No model training, no template setup, no per-vendor configuration. Define the schema you want once and you're done.

1

Define your schema

Describe the fields you want as JSON Schema, Zod, Pydantic, or plain English. Acme enforces it strictly - every response matches the shape you asked for.

2

POST your document

PDF, image, scan, screenshot, even a phone photo. We handle deskewing, OCR, layout analysis, and field extraction in a single round-trip.

3

Pipe the result downstream

Get back typed JSON with per-field confidence scores and bounding boxes. Route to your ERP, ledger, or workflow - programmatically, no human in the loop.

THE BACKSTORY

Why we built Acme.

Every company that processes documents at scale eventually runs into the same problem: extract structured data from unstructured paper. It sounds solvable until you actually try.

Most teams start with a regex. Then a template engine. Then a fine-tuned vision model. Then a human-in-the-loop review queue. Six months and a million dollars later, they have a system that works for 80% of their documents and breaks every time a vendor redesigns their letterhead.

The shape of the problem

Document understanding is not a single problem - it's a stack of them. You need OCR good enough to handle phone-camera photos. Layout analysis that survives skew, rotation, and multi-column text. A model that knows the difference between an invoice number and a purchase order. And, above all, output that matches your schema exactly, every time, so the data can flow into your ledger without manual review.

Acme is the result of two years building this stack from scratch. We don't wrap a foundation model and pray. We trained our own document encoder, our own layout model, and our own constrained-decoding system that guarantees the output matches your schema. Each of these is best-in-class on its own benchmark; together, they're what makes 99.4% field-level accuracy possible.

What we mean by "no templates"

Most document AI products require you to draw bounding boxes for every new vendor. Acme doesn't. You give us a schema once - what fields you want, what types they should be - and we figure out where they live, no matter the layout. A new invoice format from a new vendor in a new language? Same endpoint, same code path, same accuracy.

What's next

We're shipping Acme Webhooks next month - process documents as they land in S3, Gmail, or Drive, and POST the parsed JSON to your endpoint of choice. No polling. No cron jobs. Just events.

If you process more than ~10K documents a month and you're tired of duct-taping pipelines together, talk to us. We've probably already solved the problem you're about to spend a quarter on.
